## Problem Title
SIH 25010: Smart Crop Advisory System for Small and Marginal Farmers
## Problem Description
A majority of small and marginal farmers in India rely on traditional knowledge, local shopkeepers, or guesswork for crop selection, pest control, and fertilizer use. They lack access to personalized, real-time advisory services that account for soil type, weather conditions, and crop history. This often leads to poor yield, excessive input costs, and environmental degradation due to overuse of chemicals. Language barriers, low digital literacy, and absence of localized tools further limit their access to modern agri-tech resources.

Impact / Why this problem needs to be solved

Helping small farmers make informed decisions can significantly increase productivity, reduce costs, and improve livelihoods. It also contributes to sustainable farming practices, food security, and environmental conservation. A smart advisory solution can empower farmers with scientific insights in their native language and reduce dependency on unreliable third-party advice.

Expected Outcomes

• A multilingual, AI-based mobile app or chatbot that provides real-time, location-specific crop advisory.
• Soil health recommendations and fertilizer guidance.
• Weather-based alerts and predictive insights.
• Pest/disease detection via image uploads.
• Market price tracking.
• Voice support for low-literate users.
• Feedback and usage data collection for continuous improvement.

Relevant Stakeholders / Beneficiaries

• Small and marginal farmers
• Agricultural extension officers
• Government agriculture departments
• NGOs and cooperatives
• Agri-tech startups

Supporting Data

• 86% of Indian farmers are small or marginal (NABARD Report, 2022).
• Studies show ICT-based advisories can increase crop yield by 20–30%.

##  Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is an intelligent, farmer-centric platform called *AgriMitra*, designed to simplify agricultural decision-making for small and marginal farmers.  
It integrates crop planning, soil diagnostics, weather forecasting, pest alerts, and market insights into a single, accessible ecosystem.

- Offers *location-specific crop recommendations* by analyzing soil health and weather conditions.  
- Tackles everyday farming challenges like erratic rainfall, pest infestations, and fluctuating market rates.  
- *Standout feature*: uses AI to detect crop diseases from images and delivers advice in multiple Indian languages via voice and text.  
- Designed to be inclusive—farmers without smartphones can access services through *USSD codes and IVR calls*, ensuring no one is left behind.

## Technical Approach

The system is built on a distributed microservices architecture, where each functional module—crop advisory, pest analysis, weather insights, and market data—is independently deployed and orchestrated through a centralized API gateway. This ensures scalability, fault tolerance, and seamless integration across services.

- *Technologies*  
  - **Backend**: Python (FastAPI or Django REST Framework) for efficient data handling and service routing  
  - **Frontend**: React.js with Material UI for a clean, responsive user interface  
  - **Databases**: PostgreSQL for relational data, MongoDB for flexible document storage, Redis for caching, and cloud buckets for image uploads  
  - **AI/ML**: TensorFlow-based models for pest identification and crop suitability analysis using image and sensor inputs  
  - **APIs**: Integrated feeds from IMD (weather), eNAM (market prices), and ISRO Bhuvan (soil and satellite data)

This modular setup allows for rapid deployment, easy updates, and region-specific customization—making it ideal for diverse agricultural zones across India.

## Methodology

1. Farmers initiate a query using the *AgriMitra mobile app or dial-in USSD/IVR service*.  
2. The input is passed through a secure *API Gateway*, which handles request validation and directs it to the appropriate module.  
3. Based on the query type, the system routes it to the relevant *microservice*—whether it's crop planning, pest analysis, weather tracking, or market updates.  
4. Each microservice interacts with its dedicated *data repository* to fetch or store contextual information.  
5. The final advisory is compiled and delivered back to the farmer in a *localized, easy-to-understand format*—either as voice, text, or visual alerts.

## Impact and Benefits

###  Impact on Farmers  
Farmers receive timely, location-specific guidance that helps them make informed decisions about crop selection, pest control, and irrigation.  
This reduces guesswork and improves productivity, even for those with limited access to traditional extension services.

### Social Benefits  
Empowers rural communities by democratizing access to agricultural knowledge.  
Farmers become less reliant on middlemen and local dealers, fostering self-reliance and trust in digital advisory systems.

### Economic Benefits  
Optimized use of inputs like fertilizers and pesticides leads to cost savings.  
Better planning and market awareness help farmers sell at the right time and place, improving overall income.

### Environmental Benefits  
Promotes sustainable farming by reducing chemical overuse and encouraging organic alternatives.  
Early pest and disease alerts prevent widespread crop damage, preserving soil health and biodiversity.
